Our Story

বাংলা এখানে।
Bengal is here.

It started with homesickness.

When we moved from Kolkata to Bangalore, the city offered everything — opportunity, energy, warmth. But there was one thing no restaurant could give: the taste of home.

Bengalis in Bangalore know the feeling well. You crave sorshe paturi on a random weekend. You miss the Sunday kosha mangsho that would slow-cook for hours while you read the newspaper. You want the simple comfort of luchi-chholar dal on a lazy morning.

Bangla Ekhane was born from that longing — and from one simple belief: great Bengali food doesn't need to be fancy. It needs to be honest. Made with the right mustard oil, the right ratio of spices, and the patience that only comes from truly caring.

Soon, the kitchen began to travel. We set up pop-up stalls in apartment communities across East Bangalore, and through Durga Puja, so neighbours could find a plate of home just downstairs.

Then families started inviting us into their homes. A griha prabesh puja that called for khichuri and beguni as bhog. A housewarming lunch with chingri malaikari and kosha mangsho. A Matsya mukh, where a little one tasted fish for the first time. A house party that ran late on egg devils and fish fingers. Every one of them was cooked the same way we cook at the restaurant — fresh and homely, with nothing held back.

So here we are. In the heart of Bangalore — at our table at CSK Mall, in your society’s courtyard, or in your own home on the days that matter most — cooking the way our mothers and grandmothers taught us. With love, without shortcuts.

Questions? We've Got Answers

Bengali food in Gunjur, delivery & catering

Where is Bangla Ekhane?

Bangla Ekhane is a Bengali restaurant at CSK Mall, Gunjur, Bangalore 560087, close to Varthur, Whitefield and Sarjapur Road. We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00 PM to 10:30 PM, and closed on Mondays.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes. Order Bengali food for delivery on Swiggy, Zomato or Ownly, or message us on WhatsApp at +91 82403 80848 to order takeaway.

Do you do Bengali catering for events?

Yes. We cater pujas, housewarmings, house parties and other private events across Bangalore, with or without service staff. Message us on WhatsApp with the date, number of guests and location for a quote.

Which Bengali dishes do you serve?

Luchi and kachori combos, Kosha Mangsho (mutton kosha), Chicken Kosha, Kolkata biryani, fish dishes with Ilish (hilsa), Bhetki, Pabda, Rui and Katla, Prawn Malaikari, veg dishes, thalis and sweets like Rosogolla and Nabadwip Mishti Doi.

Can I book a table?

Yes, you can book a table on District, or simply walk in at CSK Mall, Gunjur.
